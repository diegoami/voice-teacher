How to judge

Each sentence is spoken by several voices, labelled A, B, C… in a random order. You are not told which provider is which.

Listen only to the words listed under the sentence. Press ✓ right if every listed word is stressed as marked. Press ✗ wrong if any is not, then tick which. Tick another word if a different word is clearly wrong. Press ? unclear if you cannot tell.

Every click is saved on the computer straight away. You can stop and come back later.
