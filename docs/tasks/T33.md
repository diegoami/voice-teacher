## Scope

Let the learner explicitly mark a lesson complete, reopen it and choose the
next lesson. A learner decision controls progression independently of item
practice evidence. Preserve imported source/course position and provenance;
do not require repeating completed historical sessions to seed the new app.
Keep weak expressions eligible for targeted future review without reopening
the whole lesson automatically. Display the distinction between learner
completion and item coverage plainly.

## Done when

1. Authenticated owner-bound completion/reopen actions are durable and idempotent
   across retries, refresh, restart and private progress export/restore. Different
   learners, sources, courses and pinned lesson identities stay separate.
2. The app provides completion, reopen and actionable next-lesson controls.
   Completion advances the recommendation even with uncovered items; reopening
   allows revisiting. Historical source position remains visible and reusable
   without fabricating learner turns, independent use, mastery or pronunciation.
3. Existing item coverage, evidence history and weak-item review remain intact.
   Automated API/browser tests cover successful actions, lost-response retry,
   undo/reopen, restart, ownership and coexistence with historical import.

## Hazards

A learner completion decision must not relabel cued practice as independent,
erase corrections or silently retire weak expressions. Legacy "Covered" is
source-specific course position, not proof of mastery. Do not conflate stopped
sessions with completed lessons or let a model decide on behalf of the learner.
