# Reading list — POC 1

Thirty-two sentences from `pimsleur-review/REVIEW_MATERIAL.md` in
learnukrainian (Session 4, directions, plus two from Sessions 0 and 1).
Lines 1–12 are the Session 4A opening, repeated in both 4A voice runs.
Lines 1–14 (marked `4A` in the source of this file) are the ones Claude
voice mode misrecognised on 2026-09-25, almost all into Russian spelling.
Stress marks are there to help you read; the script scores against the
plain spelling.

## How to record

1. Open this file on the computer screen (or print it). Record with the
   phone you use for voice lessons, with its built-in recorder app (iPhone:
   Voice Memos; Android: Recorder / Voice Recorder). Keep the default format;
   `.m4a` is best, because every provider accepts it.
2. Quiet room, phone on the table about 30 cm from your mouth, as in a
   lesson. No headset unless you use one in lessons.
3. Read every sentence once, in order, at the pace you use when you repeat
   after the teacher. Do not read the numbers. Leave about two seconds of
   silence between sentences.
4. If you misread or stumble, do not stop and do not start the sentence
   again: carry on with the next one. A slip costs every provider the same,
   and a restart adds words none of them can match.
5. One file for the whole list, about three minutes. Name it
   `reading-1.m4a` (a second take on another day: `reading-2.m4a`).
