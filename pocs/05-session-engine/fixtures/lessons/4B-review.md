# 4B — Getting to the centre by transport: targets to check

From `2026-09-26-session-4B` in learnukrainian. The teacher does not see a step's targets until you have tried the step; it uses them to judge your answer and, after two failed tries, says the first one. Check that each is what you would be expected to say, and that the stress marks are right.
