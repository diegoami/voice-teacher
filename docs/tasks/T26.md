## Scope

Make learner recording retention an explicit setting, with a retention duration and cleanup procedure separate from immutable progress events. Keep transient speech uploads private and remove them when processing/retention rules allow. Ensure source snapshots and transcripts are only exported to the verified private destination.

## Done when

1. Retention disabled leaves no learner recording after its transient processing lifetime; enabled retention keeps only the configured private assets and expires them under the documented policy.
2. Cross-learner/unauthorized audio requests and path traversal fail, and exports/logs exclude raw audio and secrets.
3. Cleanup preserves event history and marks expired audio references intelligibly; the UI explains retention and the operator can verify the destination privacy.

## Hazards

Deleting retained audio must not erase evidence events; conversely, append-only event history is not a reason to keep raw audio indefinitely.
