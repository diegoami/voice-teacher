## Scope

Extract supported observations and corrections from free dialogue using structured output with turn references. Honour immediate versus end-of-session feedback preferences. Link known curriculum items explicitly; keep new expressions private until deliberately added to the review queue.

## Done when

1. Accepted observations reference an actual learner turn and preserve original wording, preferred form and reason when supported; unsupported or fabricated references are rejected.
2. Known item use can contribute item evidence, but open conversation cannot automatically complete a course unit; recognition uncertainty remains separately labelled.
3. Reprocessing after failure does not duplicate observations, and adding a personal review target preserves provenance without modifying lesson or vocabulary repositories.

## Hazards

The dialogue model must not grade mastery, infer pronunciation from text or turn every unfamiliar word into a curriculum edit.
