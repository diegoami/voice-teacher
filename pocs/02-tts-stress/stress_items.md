# Stress items — POC 2

Fifteen sentences, each with the words whose stress is judged (`targets`,
in the source of this file). Stress marks are an acute accent after the
stressed vowel, as in the learnukrainian material.

- 1–10: lines of Session 4A (`REVIEW_MATERIAL.md` and the 4A package in
  learnukrainian), with their stress marks, except `ліво́руч`: the
  material marks `лі́воруч`, but the Academic Dictionary (СУМ-20) gives
  `ліво́руч`, like `право́руч`. Found by the listener during the test.
- 11–15: written for this POC. Each holds a word whose Ukrainian stress
  differs from the Russian one a model may have learnt (`одина́дцять`,
  `чотирна́дцять`, `дочка́`, `легки́й`, `кропива́`), a prefix-stressed form
  (`ви́хід`), or the homograph pair `за́мок` (castle) / `замо́к` (lock), which
  plain spelling cannot tell apart.
