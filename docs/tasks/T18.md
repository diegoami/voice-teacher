## Scope

Display recent sessions, observed expressions, corrections, assistance labels, course position and sync state. Compute course coverage from explicit criteria and retain independent sequences for different courses. Provide evidence drill-down to the supporting turn without invented learner scores.

## Done when

1. A synthetic multi-source history displays correct separate course positions and original/later practice provenance, including repeated versus independent attempts.
2. A stopped/partial session remains visible; free observations and modelling do not silently become independent recall or mastery.
3. Locally saved, exported and pushed states remain distinct after a failed/retried sync, and every displayed correction links to its supporting evidence.
