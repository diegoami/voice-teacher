## Scope

Make guided conversation independent of mandatory opening repetition. Keep task-card/spoken-task options, staged corrections, later-step handling and delayed recall. Represent the current two sittings as saved activities and support a combined routine without imposing a 50-turn sitting.

## Done when

1. A guided-only scenario and a combined routine follow their planned goals; accepted variants, social replies, skipped later steps and staged help preserve the intended evidence labels.
2. Pre-attempt dialogue inputs exclude hidden targets; post-attempt judging can receive targets and intentional modelling records that help.
3. Activities close at stable boundaries and resume with identical next goal/help state after restart; disabling cards does not silently enable answer leakage.

## Verification

Planned checks, not commands claimed to exist or pass today. Resolve paths and
required manual participation at main-session preflight before dispatch.

```text

```

The PR records one result per numbered criterion, relevant baseline/regression
checks and any manual evidence. Independent exact-head review precedes merge.

## Hazards

Target hiding applies to the generator before an attempt, not to legitimate judging afterwards. Do not equate a completed activity with mastery.
